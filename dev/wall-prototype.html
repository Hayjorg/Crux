How it works: log a climbing session, a workout or a rest day and your climber moves up one hold. One hold a day, however much you log. Workouts earn a star. Missing a day ends the streak, but you never lose holds you've climbed. Every 7th hold is a ledge with a reward.

This is a prototype with example history. The buttons under "Prototype" let you jump days ahead to try streaks, misses and ledges. Nothing here touches the real Crux app.
