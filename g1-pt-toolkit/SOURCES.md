# Sources and verification notes

Checked 2026-10-13. This lists where each NASM-informed claim in the toolkit comes from, how well it is supported,
and what was changed as a result.

## How this was checked, and its limits

- **I could not open nasm.org directly.** The build environment's network policy refused `nasm.org` and
  `blog.nasm.org` (and every other outside site). Each claim below was checked with a web-search tool restricted to NASM's
  own domains, which retrieves and summarises the page. So "supported" here means *NASM's page, as retrieved by that
  tool, says this*, not that the full page was read line by line. The links are there so a person can read them.
- **NASM's blog articles differ from each other in detail** (muscle lists, which view a compensation is read from),
  and several are 2 to 10 years old. Where they disagree, the toolkit uses the most conservative wording.
- NASM's overactive/underactive framework is NASM's training model. The search found no independent research
  showing it improves outcomes, and research on some links is mixed (for example calf involvement in forward lean).
  The toolkit therefore calls the reference table *starting hypotheses*, as NASM's own framing does.
- Anyone who holds NASM materials should still read the current textbook against this. Several items below say so.

## Screening forms (PAR-Q+)

- NASM presents the PAR-Q+ as the pre-participation screen for trainers:
  [The PAR-Q+: A Trainer's Guide to Pre-Participation Screening](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/blog/fitness/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-par-q).
- The PAR-Q+ Collaboration's [terms](https://eparmedx.com/about/terms-and-conditions/) and the form itself
  ([2025 PAR-Q+](https://eparmedx.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/PARQPlus2025Fillable.pdf)) say the whole questionnaire
  must be used with **no changes**, and it may not be built into electronic surveys or derivative materials without
  the Collaboration's written consent.
- So: intake section 5 is a short custom "comfort check", **not** the PAR-Q+, and the toolkit doesn't reproduce PAR-Q+
  wording. If G1 adopts the PAR-Q+, it should attach the official unaltered form (it isn't built into these pages).
  The intake now has a placeholder for G1's screening form and clearance rule.

## What is not from NASM

These parts are ordinary template content added for G1 and are not NASM protocols: the intake questions, the resting
measures, the baseline snapshot (plank, push-up, sit-to-stand, easy cardio, climbing check), the climbing add-ons
(knee-to-wall, trunk rotation, overhead reach, supported hang), the goals tracker layout (habit grid, measures,
check-ins), and the post-session note and message. The sheets label the climbing add-ons accordingly.
