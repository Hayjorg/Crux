SMART goals

Specific · Measurable · Attainable · Realistic · Time-bound. Aim for one main goal and up to two supporting goals. Outcome = the result you want. Performance = a skill or number you can test. Process = a habit you control. Pair an outcome goal with a process goal. Targets are aims, not promises.
