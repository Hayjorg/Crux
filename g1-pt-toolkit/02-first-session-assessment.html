First-Session Assessment

Trainer use. A NASM-informed starting point for programming, not a medical exam or diagnosis.

Draft v0.1Pending G1 management approval

Scope. Personal trainers observe movement to plan exercise. We don't diagnose, treat or rehabilitate injuries. Describe what you see in plain words (for example "left shoulder sits higher"), and refer out when something is outside our scope. Bodies differ: adapt or skip any screen that doesn't suit the client, and treat differences as information, not faults. Keep nutrition advice general, and suggest a qualified professional for medical or eating concerns.
