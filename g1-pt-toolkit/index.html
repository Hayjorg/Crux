Onboarding Toolkit

Four short documents for a new client's first weeks. Print them, or save each as a PDF. Draft v0.1, pending G1 management approval.

1 · Client Intake Questionnaire Client fills in, before session one · 2 pages

Goals, training and climbing background, a self-reported comfort check, and coaching preferences. Waiver, pricing, payment and cancellation are placeholders.

2 · First-Session Assessment Trainer fills in, during session one · 3 pages

Check-in, stop-and-refer reminders, NASM-informed movement observations, a light baseline, and a starting plan. Observation for programming only, never a diagnosis.

3 · Goals and Progress Tracker Trainer and client together, end of session one, then every ~4 weeks · 2 pages

SMART goals, weekly habits, measures at weeks 4, 8 and 12, training blocks, and check-in notes.

4 · Post-Session Follow-Up Trainer, after every session · 2 pages

A two-minute session note, then a copy-ready message to the client with homework and the next session.

Before using with real clients

Placeholders pending G1 management approval:

  • Waiver and liability release
  • Pricing, packages and payment terms
  • Cancellation and late policy
  • How client health information is stored, protected and shared
  • Under-18s, guardian consent and medical-clearance rules
  • Pre-activity screening form (the official PAR-Q+ must be used unaltered)
  • Emergency action plan (first aid, AED), incident process, and the resting heart rate, blood pressure and body-measurement policy

Good to know

  • Nothing typed into these pages is saved or sent. Fields clear when you reload, so print or save the PDF first.
  • Don't enter real client personal or health information until G1 approves how it is stored.
  • NASM-informed, not affiliated with or endorsed by NASM. Movement observations are for exercise programming, not medical diagnosis.
