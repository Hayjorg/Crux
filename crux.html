    How the Wall works

    • Log a day, climb a hold. A session, a workout or a rest day all count. Workouts earn a star.
    • Every hold unlocks something. Every 7th is a ledge with a bigger prize.
    • Miss a day and your streak resets, but you never lose holds.
    • Tap a hold to see its day, or your climber to see their moves.

      Safety, privacy & terms

      Climbing is dangerous

      Climbing, bouldering, and hangboard or finger training carry an inherent risk of serious injury, including falls, tendon and pulley injuries, and injuries that can be permanent or fatal. Crux is a timer and logbook. It doesn't supervise, spot, or assess you, and it can't tell whether a climb, a fall zone, or a workout is safe for you.

      Use proper equipment and padding, get instruction from a qualified person, follow your gym's rules, and stop if something hurts.

      Not medical or coaching advice

      Workout descriptions and timer presets are general information for experienced climbers, not personal coaching, medical, or physical-therapy advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a training program, especially if you're new to finger training, returning from injury, or have a health condition. Levels, badges, and streaks are for fun, not a measure of what you're ready for.

      Kids

      Anyone under 18 should use Crux, and climb or train, only with a parent or guardian's permission and appropriate supervision.

      Your data

      There's no account, no ads, and no analytics. Your sessions, notes, and progress are saved only in this browser on this device, and nobody else, including the maker of Crux, can see them. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling deletes them, so use Export backup in Settings to keep a copy.

      The coach is optional. Only when you tap a coach button, the climbing log it needs (that session or project, plus a few recent sessions) is sent to Claude on your own Claude account to write the answer, and it uses your Claude usage. The coach's tips are general suggestions, not professional coaching or medical advice.

      Photos and videos you add are stored with the copy of Crux you're using, so treat them as you would anything you upload. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when those fonts load.
